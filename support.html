常見問題

Q:我的進度不見了 / 換手機後進度沒跟著過來?
遊戲進度只存在你的裝置本機(沒有帳號、沒有雲端存檔),所以: 刪除應用程式會一併刪掉進度,換裝置也不會自動搬過去。 這是刻意的設計 —— 不要帳號才能做到「零個資收集」(見隱私權政策)。 ⚠ 目前沒有手動備份或轉移進度的功能。
Q:怎麼調音量、怎麼靜音?
星域選單 → 選一個星域 → 在關卡選擇頁按 SPACE(或點畫面下排的「操作說明」), 該頁最下面一列就是音量:← → 調主音量、按 1 靜音; 音量刻度也可以直接點你要的位置。
Q:怎麼切換中文 / 英文?
同一頁(操作說明頁)底列的「2 介面文字」——按 2 在 中文 / English 之間切換, 重新啟動遊戲後生效(切了之後那一列會亮黃字提醒你)。
第一次玩會依你的系統語言自動挑;之後只聽你自己切的,不會被系統語言改回去。
Q:玩到一半想離開這一局,要怎麼退出?
遊戲中螢幕最右上角(分數那一條的頂端)有一顆「離開」——點兩下才會退: 第一下會跳出「再點一次離開本局」,兩秒內再點才真的退出。 這樣設計是為了讓誤觸不會毀掉你正在打的一局。
退出是一次退一層:遊戲 → 關卡頁 → 星域選單 → 標題。
Q:抽卡的機率是多少?會不會抽到重複的?
抽卡用的是遊戲內免費取得的反物質燃料(打分結算入帳、或看獎勵廣告), 不能用真錢買、也不能換成真錢 —— 這一款沒有任何付費購買入口。
手機版不提供逐件機率表頁面。稀有度分三級(普通 / 進階 / 傳奇), 傳奇最稀有;抽到已有的部件會累加數量(部件是消耗品,不是永久裝備)。
Q:廣告會不會在我打球的時候跳出來?
不會。本作沒有橫幅廣告、沒有插頁廣告 —— 所有廣告都是你自己按了才播的獎勵式廣告(按鈕上會寫明看完給什麼)。 中途離開廣告只是拿不到那份獎勵,不會有其他後果。
不想看任何廣告也完全玩得下去:所有廣告獎勵都是加分項,不是通關必需品。
Q:我卡在某一關過不去,是不是壞了?
每一關開打前都有一張開場簡報,會把「這一關要打什麼」套上光圈指給你看(點畫面關掉,不佔遊戲時間)。 遊戲中右欄也一直寫著當前目標。三張檯的通關鏈是同一條: 擊入黑洞充能 → 蟲洞轉金 → 射入蟲洞逃出生天。
⚠ 若蟲洞看起來該轉金卻還是紫色,通常是因為你正在多球中(結算會等多球結束才兌現)。 等球數回到一顆再看。若你確定不是這個情況,請寄信給我並附截圖。
Q:有沒有無盡模式 / 打完關卡之後還有什麼可以玩?
有兩個:①「深淵下探」(關卡選擇頁底列)——一直往下潛,每 3 層三選一拿強化, 死了就結束、但生涯最深深度永久留在紀錄裡。②星圖循環——破關後續玩, 敵人變硬、任務變長、分數係數變高。

FAQ

Q: My progress is gone / it didn't follow me to a new phone.
Progress is stored only on your device — there are no accounts and no cloud saves. So deleting the app deletes your progress, and switching devices does not carry it over. That is deliberate: having no account is what makes "collects no personal data" true (see the Privacy Policy). ⚠ There is currently no manual backup or transfer feature.
Q: How do I change the volume or mute the game?
Star map → pick a region → on the level select screen press SPACE (or tap "Controls" on the bottom row). The bottom line of that page is the volume: ← → adjusts master volume, 1 mutes. You can also tap the meter directly.
Q: How do I switch between English and Chinese?
On that same Controls page, the bottom row has "2 Language" — press 2 to switch between 中文 and English. It takes effect after you restart the game (the row turns yellow to remind you).
On a first run the game picks a language from your system settings; after that it only follows your own choice.
Q: How do I quit a run in progress?
During play there is an exit control in the top-right corner of the screen (at the top of the score column). You must tap it twice: the first tap shows "tap again to leave this run", and only a second tap within two seconds actually exits. That way a stray touch cannot destroy the ball you are playing.
Exiting goes one layer at a time: game → level select → star map → title.
Q: What are the gacha odds? Are they disclosed?
Fully disclosed, in-game: from the star map press SPACE to open the Loadout Bay, then press ENTER for the odds table — all 12 parts listed individually with their percentages, plus the raw weights so you can check the arithmetic.
⚠ Those numbers are computed at runtime from the same weight table the draw itself reads, not typed in by hand, so the displayed odds can never drift from the real ones.
Draws cost antimatter fuel, which is only ever earned in-game (from scoring, or by watching a rewarded ad). It cannot be bought with real money and cannot be cashed out.
Q: Will ads interrupt me mid-ball?
No. There are no banners and no interstitials. Every ad is a rewarded ad that only plays when you tap it, and the button always states the reward up front. Leaving an ad early just means no reward.
You can also ignore ads entirely: every ad reward is a bonus, never a requirement for finishing a level.
Q: I'm stuck on a level — is it broken?
Every level opens with a briefing that rings the targets you need to hit (tap to dismiss; it does not eat your play time). The right-hand column also shows the current objective at all times. All three tables share one escape chain: charge the black hole → the wormhole turns gold → shoot it to escape.
⚠ If the wormhole looks like it should be gold but is still violet, you are usually in multiball — the clear is held until multiball ends. Wait until you are down to one ball. If that isn't it, email me with a screenshot.
Q: Is there an endless mode? Anything after the campaign?
Two things: (1) Abyss Dive (bottom row of level select) — descend as far as you can, pick one of three upgrades every 3 layers, and when you're out of balls the run ends but your deepest depth is kept forever. (2) Star-map loops — keep playing after the campaign with tougher enemies, longer missions and a higher score multiplier.

What this game does not have (honest list)

  • Cloud saves / accounts / cross-device sync — no, and not planned (see Privacy Policy).
  • Online leaderboards / multiplayer / friends / chat — no, fully single-player and offline.
  • In-app purchases — none. In-game currency is not sold and cannot be cashed out.
  • Manual save backup or transfer — no.
