隱私權政策(繁體中文)

一句話:SINGULARC 是一款單機遊戲。遊戲本身不連網、不需要帳號、不收集任何個人資料, 你的進度只存在你自己的裝置上。唯一會處理資料的是顯示獎勵式廣告的 Google AdMob, 而那些廣告永遠要你主動點才會播。

1. 我們是誰

本應用程式「SINGULARC」由 Oceantis (LO HUNG CHIN) 開發與發行。 隱私相關問題請聯絡:oceantis.support@gmail.com

2. 我們收集哪些資料

2.1 遊戲本體:不收集

遊戲程式不含任何網路連線功能,也沒有帳號、登入、雲端存檔、排行榜或社群功能。 我們不會收集、傳輸或儲存你的任何個人資料。

你的遊戲進度(分數、生涯紀錄、反物質燃料、倉庫與出擊配置、音量與語言設定) 以純文字檔只儲存在你的裝置本機,不會上傳到任何伺服器,我們也讀不到它。

2.2 遊戲內的統計數字:純本機

遊戲內若顯示任何次數統計(例如你看過幾次廣告、開過幾個寶箱),那些數字 只是本機存檔裡的計數器,用來驅動遊戲規則,不會傳送到任何地方, 也不會與任何識別碼關聯。

2.3 唯一的第三方:Google AdMob(廣告)

本應用使用 Google AdMob 顯示獎勵式廣告(rewarded ads)。 這些廣告的特性是:

  • 全部由你主動觸發 —— 一定是你自己按了畫面上寫明獎勵的按鈕才會播。
  • 沒有橫幅廣告、沒有插頁廣告,不會在你打球的中途跳出來打斷你。
  • 中途離開廣告 = 不會拿到獎勵,但也不會有任何其他後果。

為了投放廣告,Google AdMob 可能收集與處理下列資料:

資料用途
廣告識別碼(iOS 的 IDFA / Android 的廣告 ID)廣告投放、頻率控管、成效衡量
裝置與系統資訊(機型、作業系統版本、語言、國別等)廣告投放、相容性
由 IP 位址推得的粗略位置(國家/地區層級)廣告投放、詐欺防治
廣告互動事件(曝光、觀看完成、點擊)成效衡量、計費
⚠ 請注意:上述資料是由 Google 直接收集與處理,依照 Google 隱私權政策 與 Google 廣告技術政策 辦理。 我們本身不會取得、不會儲存、也不會轉售這些資料,我們只看得到 Google 後台的匯總統計 (例如「今天總共播了幾支廣告」),那些數字無法對應到任何個別使用者。

3. 我們不會做的事

  • 不收集姓名、email、電話、通訊錄、行事曆、照片、檔案、麥克風、相機、 精確位置、健康或財務資料。
  • 不要求你註冊帳號,也沒有帳號可以註冊。
  • 不販售或出租你的資料。
  • 不做應用程式內購買(IAP)。遊戲內的貨幣不能用真錢購買,也不能兌換成真錢。
  • 不屬於兒童類目(Kids Category),不針對兒童投放廣告。
  • 不使用任何自建的分析或追蹤 SDK。

4. 你的選擇與控制權

  • iOS 追蹤:首次啟動時若出現「允許追蹤」的系統詢問,選不允許即可; 之後也可在「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」隨時關閉。 關閉後廣告照樣能看、獎勵照樣拿得到,只是廣告的個人化程度降低。
  • Android:可在「設定 → Google → 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID。
  • 歐洲:本應用目前不在歐洲地區供應(含歐盟、英國、瑞士)。
  • 刪除全部資料:刪除本應用程式,即會一併刪除所有本機儲存的遊戲資料。 由於我們手上沒有你的資料,不需要(也無法)向我們提出刪除請求。

5. 兒童隱私

本應用不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計,也不會在知情的情況下收集其個人資料。 本應用未申請兒童類目,並已在廣告後台將廣告內容分級設定為不高於本應用的年齡分級。 若你認為有兒童的資料被誤收,請來信 oceantis.support@gmail.com。

6. 資料保存與安全

我們不持有你的任何資料,因此沒有保存期限可言。本機存檔的安全性由你的裝置本身 (作業系統的沙箱機制)保護。Google AdMob 端的保存期限依 Google 自己的政策辦理。

7. 政策變更

本政策若有變更,會更新本頁最上方的「最後更新」日期。若變更涉及資料收集方式的實質改變 (例如未來新增應用程式內購買或其他第三方 SDK),我們會在遊戲內或商店頁的更新說明中告知。

8. 聯絡我們

oceantis.support@gmail.com

Privacy Policy (English)

In one sentence: SINGULARC is an offline single-player game. The game itself has no network code, no accounts, and collects no personal data — your progress lives only on your own device. The only party that processes any data is Google AdMob, which serves the rewarded ads, and those ads only ever play when you choose to watch one.

1. Who we are

SINGULARC is developed and published by Oceantis (LO HUNG CHIN). For privacy enquiries: oceantis.support@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

2.1 The game itself: none

The game contains no networking code and has no accounts, sign-in, cloud saves, leaderboards or social features. We do not collect, transmit or store any personal data.

Your progress (scores, career records, antimatter fuel, inventory and loadout, volume and language settings) is written to a plain-text file stored only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere, and we cannot read it.

2.2 In-game counters: local only

Any counters shown in-game (for example how many ads you have watched, or how many chests you have opened) are local save-file counters that drive game rules. They are never transmitted anywhere and are not linked to any identifier.

2.3 The only third party: Google AdMob (advertising)

This app uses Google AdMob to serve rewarded ads. Specifically:

  • Every ad is opt-in — one only plays after you tap a button that states the reward.
  • No banner ads and no interstitials; nothing interrupts you mid-ball.
  • Leaving an ad early simply means no reward. There is no other consequence.

To serve those ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / Advertising ID on Android)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement
Device and system information (model, OS version, language, country)Ad delivery, compatibility
Coarse location inferred from IP address (country/region level)Ad delivery, fraud prevention
Ad interaction events (impression, completed view, click)Measurement, billing
⚠ Please note: the data above is collected and processed directly by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies. We never receive, store or resell it. We only see aggregate figures in the AdMob dashboard (such as total ads served today), which cannot be traced to any individual user.

3. What we never do

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise location, health or financial data.
  • We do not ask you to register an account — there is no account system at all.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We have no in-app purchases. In-game currency cannot be bought with real money and cannot be cashed out.
  • This app is not in the Kids Category and does not target advertising at children.
  • We use no first-party analytics or tracking SDK.

4. Your choices

  • iOS tracking: if the system asks you to allow tracking, choosing Ask App Not to Track is fine; you can also change it later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads still work and rewards are still granted — only ad personalisation is reduced.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Europe: this app is currently not available in Europe (including the EU, the UK and Switzerland).
  • Deleting everything: deleting the app deletes all locally stored game data. Because we hold no data about you, there is no deletion request to send us (and none we could act on).

5. Children's privacy

This app is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. The app is not in the Kids Category, and the maximum ad content rating in our ad dashboard is configured no higher than the app's own age rating. If you believe a child's data has been collected in error, contact oceantis.support@gmail.com.

6. Retention and security

We hold none of your data, so no retention period applies on our side. Your local save file is protected by your device's own operating-system sandbox. Retention on Google's side is governed by Google's own policies.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. If a change materially affects data collection (for example adding in-app purchases or another third-party SDK), we will say so in the in-game or store release notes.

8. Contact

oceantis.support@gmail.com
